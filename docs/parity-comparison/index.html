ChargePoint

biggest gaps
ANDROID (approved — offer-state design inspector)
iOS (current — offer state)
  • Fixed orange brand rail ~37%, "Auto" pill, Accept / Snooze keys, account, a live countdown exists
  • Hero emphasis: Android leads with the big countdown clock ("13:53" + "to accept" + "respond by 6:37 PM"); iOS leads with the word "Your turn" and shows the timer small ("2:44")
  • Right-panel body: Android shows "go to level P3 / SEA41-29" (which charger/floor to go to); iOS shows the site name + account instead
  • Floor-level stats: Android lists per-level availability (P2–P6 with counts) in the Snoozed/idle states; iOS has no floor levels at all
  • Android surfaces more per-state detail (Accepting…, failed-Accept, Snoozed-by-ChargePoint variants) in the body region
Cross-cutting: the ~37% rail split now matches on all four (was 50/50). Interactions run in place on iOS (no app launch) except where iOS forbids it. Remaining non-layout walls: the system app-name label under Home-Screen widgets, and no continuous refresh-button animation — both iOS platform limits, documented in docs/PARITY-AUDIT.md.
